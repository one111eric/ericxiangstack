## Notes

### Crafting

Bow: Easiest way to craft is by using recombs for a triple t2 prefix bow.
1) Aug ilvl 82 spine bows for t2 or better flat
2) recomb 2 together. Essentially you keep doing this step until you endup with 2 bows that have 2 prefix each(suffix don't matter).
3) You'll combine 2 of those bows together (so 2 prefix with 2 prefix) - You just need 1 of each element, with 1 overlap.
For example fire+cold prefix with fire+lightning. This gives you a 1/3 chance to succeed the recomb and endup with triple t2 prefix.
4) Now with prefixes done you lock them with bench craft (prefixes cannot be changed, 2 divines)
5) Use veiled chaos orb, this will only reroll your suffix. On unveil you're looking for crit or attack speed (crit is preferable, but either work).
6) Craft the stat you didn't unveil, so crit or attack speed and you're done.

Quiver: Buy a fractured +1 arrow or T1 multi base, preferable on feathered arrow quiver. Needs to be at least ilvl 81, (ilvl 86 is ideal though)
1) Spam contempt essence until you hit T1 multi (or +1 arrow if you have a fractured multi base).
2) You now need 2 empty prefixes and 1 empty suffix. Annul if you need to, high chance to brick and if it does, back to step 1.
3) Craft cannot roll attack and use a combination of exalts/annuls until your desired life/increased damage with bow skills.
When you have "craft cannot roll attack" you wont be able to annul your +1 arrow, or multi or flat physical damage. And your only exalt outcomes are maximum life and increased damage with bows skills.

Body armour: Buy a T1 resist fractured evasion chest or spell suppression (but resist is probably a lot cheaper).
1) Spam any resist essence until you hit T1 or T2 spell suppression.
2) Use wild bristle matreon in bestiary for a cheap "suffixes cannot be changed" and then use harvest reforge life, repeat until t1 or t2 life.
